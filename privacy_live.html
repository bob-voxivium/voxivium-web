Voxivium Privacy Policy

Last Updated: July 26, 2025

1. Introduction

Welcome to Voxivium, a political accountability and transparency platform operated by Voxivium, a Public Benefit Corporation. This Privacy Policy outlines our commitment to protecting your privacy and explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our application and services ("Services"). Our guiding principle is that your personally identifiable information will not be sold or shared for commercial purposes.

Your trust is fundamental to our mission. As a Public Benefit Corporation, we are legally committed to balancing profit with our public benefit purpose of fostering greater political accountability and transparency. At Voxivium, we treat your data as a sacred trust, with a "privacy-first" approach embedded in everything we do.

By using our Services, you agree to the collection, use, and disclosure of your information as described in this Privacy Policy.

2. Information We Collect

To provide our Services, we need to collect certain information. We are committed to collecting only what is necessary and to being transparent about our data practices.

2.1. Information You Provide to Us

Account and Identity Verification Information: When you create a Voxivium account, we collect information to verify your identity and voter registration status. This may include your full name, date of birth, address, email, phone number, and the last four digits of your Social Security Number or driver's license number. This information is used for the singular purpose of verifying your identity and voter registration with third-party verification services.

Your Political Views and Beliefs (Sensitive Personal Information): The core of Voxivium's service is to allow you to express your views on legislation, federal court rulings, executive orders, and general political issues. This includes your support or opposition, the importance you place on each issue, and nuanced positions you may express. This type of data is considered Sensitive Personal Information, and we use it only to provide the core functionality of the Services you have requested.

Communications: If you contact us directly for support or other inquiries, we may receive additional information about you, such as the contents of your message and any other information you choose to provide.

2.2. Information from Politicians and Candidates

Elected officials and candidates using our platform may provide their official positions on issues, which will be made public on their profiles.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect for the following purposes:

To Provide and Maintain Our Services:

  • To verify your identity and voter registration status to ensure the integrity of our platform.
  • To create and manage your account and profile.
  • To generate your personalized "report cards" that show how your representatives' voting records align with your stated positions.
  • To generate candidate comparison reports to help you make informed decisions during elections.
  • For politicians, to provide them with their "constituent report card" based on aggregated and anonymized data.

To Improve and Personalize Our Services:

  • To offer algorithmic recommendations on your potential positions on new legislation based on your previously stated beliefs, which you can then confirm or override.
  • To understand how our users engage with the platform to improve user experience and develop new features.

To Communicate With You:

  • To send you notifications about new issues relevant to you, report card updates, and other service-related communications. You will have control over the types and frequency of these notifications.

4. How We Share and Sell Information

Our commitment is to never sell or share your personally identifiable information.

4.1. Aggregated and Anonymized Data

While we will never sell your personal information, our business model does involve licensing access to anonymized and aggregated data sets to professional users. Under some state laws, this may be considered a "sale." We provide this information with the express goal of increasing transparency and accountability in government.

  • For Politicians and Candidates: We provide politicians and candidates with access to aggregated and anonymized data on their constituents' viewpoints. This includes collective scores and report cards showing the percentage of constituents who support or oppose their stance on an issue. This data is always presented in a way that does not identify individual users.
  • For Media Accounts: Licensed media organizations receive access to aggregated and anonymized voter statistics at national, state, and local levels. This allows them to report on public sentiment without compromising individual privacy.
  • For AI Agents: To aid in the development of ethical AI, we provide licensed AI development companies and research institutions with API access to aggregated, anonymized human sentiment data. This data does not contain any personally identifiable information and is provided under strict licensing terms.

4.2. With Third-Party Service Providers

We may share your information with third-party vendors who perform services on our behalf, such as identity verification, cloud hosting, and payment processing. These service providers have access only to the information necessary to perform their functions and are contractually obligated to protect your information.

4.3. For Legal Reasons

We may disclose your information if required by law or to protect the rights, property, or safety of Voxivium, our users, or the public.

5. Data Security

We take the security of your data seriously and implement robust technical and organizational measures to protect it, including encryption, access controls, and regular security audits. While we strive to use commercially acceptable means to protect your information, no method of transmission over the Internet is 100% secure.

6. Your State Privacy Rights

Residents of certain states, such as California, Colorado, Connecticut, Utah, and Virginia, have specific rights regarding their personal information. This section describes those rights and explains how you can exercise them.

6.1. Your California Privacy Rights (CCPA/CPRA)

If you are a California resident, you have the following rights:

  • Right to Know and Access: You have the right to request that we disclose the categories and specific pieces of personal information we have collected about you, the sources of that information, the business purpose for collecting it, and the categories of third parties with whom we share it.
  • Right to Delete: You have the right to request the deletion of your personal information that we have collected, subject to certain exceptions (e.g., information needed to comply with legal obligations).
  • Right to Correct: You have the right to request the correction of inaccurate personal information we hold about you.
  • Right to Opt-Out of Sale/Sharing: You have the right to opt out of the "sale" or "sharing" of your personal information. As stated in Section 4, we do not sell or share your personally identifiable information. Our revenue model is based on licensing anonymized, aggregated statistical data. However, we honor your right to opt out of any such data practices. You can exercise this right by visiting our Do Not Sell or Share My Personal Information page.
  • Right to Limit Use of Sensitive Personal Information and Its Effect on Your Account: You have the right to limit the use and disclosure of your sensitive personal information. Please be aware that the fundamental purpose of the Voxivium platform is to process your political beliefs (your sensitive personal information) to generate your personalized representative report cards and to include your views in the collective sentiment provided to politicians, media, and other licensed users. Without the ability to process this specific information, the Services have no function to offer you. The platform is designed as a tool for this express purpose. Therefore, a request to limit the use of your sensitive personal information is a request that makes it impossible for us to provide any Services to you. We will treat a request to limit the use of your sensitive personal information as a request to permanently delete your account and all associated data.
  • Right to No Retaliation: You have the right not to be discriminated against for exercising any of your privacy rights.

California's "Shine the Light" Law: California residents also have the right to request information from us once a calendar year about the categories of personal information (if any) we disclosed to third parties for their direct marketing purposes. We do not share personally identifiable information with third parties for their direct marketing purposes.

6.2. Rights for Residents of Other States

If you are a resident of Colorado, Connecticut, Utah, or Virginia, you may have rights similar to those of California residents, including the right to access, correct, delete, and opt out of the sale of your personal data. You can exercise these rights via the methods below.

6.3. How to Exercise Your Rights

To exercise any of the rights described above, please visit the following page Your Privacy Choices. We will respond to your request consistent with applicable law.

7. Data Retention

We will retain your personal information for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide you our Services. We will also retain information as necessary to comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce our agreements.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Last Updated" date.
