Voxivium, derived from the Latin *Vox Civium* meaning "The Voice of the Citizens," is a Public Benefit Corporation (PBC) developing a political accountability and transparency platform[cite: 4, 56]. Its primary purpose is to repair the relationship between citizens and elected officials by creating a direct, data-driven line of communication that prioritizes constituent will over donor influence.

### Core Functions and Features
* **Personal Report Card**: The platform tracks the votes and public statements of a user's representatives and compares them against the user's personal stances on key issues.
* **Accountability Scoring**: Based on these comparisons, the system generates an ongoing, personalized accountability score for each politician.
* **Bridging Divides**: Voxivium focuses on specific issues rather than party rhetoric to reveal areas of common ground across the political spectrum.
* **Unbiased Information**: The platform provides multi-tiered summaries of legislation (ranging from high-level pros/cons to full legal text) to ensure voters are well-informed.
* **Candidate Comparisons**: During elections, users can generate side-by-side reports comparing candidates' stated positions directly against the user's beliefs.

### Target Users and Revenue Model
Voxivium utilizes a freemium model to ensure accessibility while maintaining financial sustainability:
* **Individual Voters**: Core functionalities, such as stating positions and receiving report cards, are free.
* **Politicians**: Paid accounts allow officeholders and candidates to view aggregated, anonymized constituent data and share their official alignment scores.
* **Media and Research**: Subscribers gain access to detailed voter sentiment statistics and unity/polarization metrics for reporting purposes.
* **AI Labs**: Voxivium offers a "Ground Truth" license for AI alignment, helping ensure artificial intelligence models reflect authentic human values.

### Corporate Philosophy and Ethics
As a Public Benefit Corporation, Voxivium is committed to "responsible capitalism" through specific internal governance standards:
* **Privacy by Design**: Individual user data is never for sale and remains anonymous.
* **Equitable Pay**: The company intends to codify a **5:1 executive-to-employee pay ratio** and a living wage commitment for all staff.
* **Profit Sharing**: Profits are shared among employees after maintaining necessary operational reserves.

### Future Vision
Voxivium plans to expand from federal tracking to state and local government levels. Future features may also include **Expert Sentiment Tracking**, which would highlight the consensus of subject-matter experts (such as scientists or academics) on specific policy mandates to further inform the public and politicians.
