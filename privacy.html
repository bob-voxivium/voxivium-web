Welcome to Voxivium. Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how Voxivium, a Public Benefit Corporation ("Voxivium," "we," "us," or "our"), collects, uses, and protects the information you provide when using the Voxivium.com website (the "Site").

1. Information We Collect

When you sign up for our waitlist, we collect the following personal information from you:

  • First Name

  • State of Residence

Your submission of this information is completely voluntary.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information you provide for the sole purpose of communicating with you about the Voxivium platform. This includes sending you updates on our development progress, notifying you when we launch, and sharing other relevant company news. We will not use your information for any other purpose without your consent.

3. Data Sharing and Security

We are committed to protecting your data. We will not sell, rent, trade, or share your personal information with any third parties for their own marketing purposes. We implement reasonable security measures to protect your information from unauthorized access, use, or disclosure.

4. Your Choices and Rights

You can opt-out of receiving our email communications at any time by clicking the "unsubscribe" link provided in the footer of every email we send. Upon unsubscribing, we will remove your information from our mailing list.

5. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. If we make changes, we will notify you by revising the "Last Updated" date at the top of this policy. We encourage you to review this policy periodically to stay informed.
