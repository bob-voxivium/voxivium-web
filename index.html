What is Voxivium?

The name Voxivium was inspired by the Latin words "Vox Civium," which translates to "The Voice of the Citizens." This name embodies our core mission: to build a government that is truly "of the people, by the people, and for the people."

Empower Your Voice

Voxivium provides a direct, transparent, and data-driven line of communication to your elected officials, ensuring they know where you stand on the issues that matter most.

Demand Accountability

Effortlessly create a "Personal Report Card" for any politician, tracking their votes against your own stance on key issues. Hold them accountable with a personalized score.

Bridge Partisan Divides

Discover surprising areas of common ground. Voxivium reveals where citizens unite across party lines, empowering unified advocacy for the policies you mutually support.

Our Commitment as a Public Benefit Corporation

Voxivium is incorporated as a Public Benefit Corporation (PBC), which legally binds us to our mission. This isn't just a promise; it's our corporate charter. Being a PBC means our success is measured not only by financial returns, but by how effectively we empower citizens and foster a more accountable government. This structure ensures that our core purpose—serving the public good—will always guide our decisions.
